# QR pairing

AnyTTY mobile apps add an endpoint when you scan a QR code or paste a pairing claim generated by the target daemon. Direct pairing does not require an account.

## Trust sequence

1. **The daemon creates a short-lived claim.** The offer is tied to its identity, the requested access, and one enrollment flow.
2. **The client scans or pastes the claim and proves its identity.** The native client stores endpoint data and credentials in platform-protected storage.
3. **The daemon issues access tied to that client.** The claim is marked as used, so it cannot be replayed.

> **Treat an unredeemed QR as a secret.** Do not place pairing URIs in logs, screenshots, issue reports, chat, or version control. Revoke a grant from the daemon if a client is lost or no longer trusted.

See the repository's [security boundary](https://github.com/anytty/anytty/blob/main/docs/SECURITY_BOUNDARY.md) for the pairing and revocation guarantees.
