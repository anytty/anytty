# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AnyTTY community free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Accept correction, focus on the technical issue, and credit others' work.
- Protect private data, credentials, and unpublished vulnerability details.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, security, and release readiness.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publication of others' private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub's repository contact mechanisms. Do not use the security advisory form unless the report concerns a product vulnerability. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior conduct, and the safety of affected people. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report.
